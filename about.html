The person behind the laser

D-Side Designs started as a hobby — a way to try out an idea and see what stuck. Then pieces started showing up around the office, coworkers started asking questions, and the questions turned into orders. Six months later, it's a business.

Ask what he's proudest of and it's not any single piece — it's the range. Leather, wood, metal, personal keepsakes, even a paintball marker have all ended up under the laser. Whether it's a $6 coaster or something that means a lot to you, every order gets the same care: careful, precise, and worth doing right.

How an order comes together

Every piece goes through the same three steps, whether it's a $6 coaster or a full custom portrait.

01

Design

You send the idea — a photo, a phrase, a "surprise me." Larry lays it out on screen and confirms it with you before anything gets cut.

02

Engrave

The laser burns the design into the material, precise down to a fraction of a millimeter. No two passes look quite the same.

03

Ready

Local orders (DeKalb / Sycamore area) are ready in 1–2 business days. Need it shipped instead? That adds a bit more time.
